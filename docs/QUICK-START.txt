AKENO COLORVISION — QUICK START
===============================

Akeno ColorVision changes the colors on your whole screen so that colors which
normally look the same (many reds, greens, browns and grays) become different
colors or different brightness. It can't cure color blindness — it helps you
tell things apart.

1. START IT
   Double-click "AkenoColorVision.exe".
   Nothing needs to be installed. Keep both .exe files in the same folder.

   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen) because the app
   is new and not code-signed. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".

2. TURN THE FILTER ON
   Click the big "Turn filter on" button.
   Shortcut anywhere (even in games):  Ctrl + Alt + Shift + F

3. MAKE IT YOURS (recommended, about 5 minutes)
   Click "Calibration" on the left and follow the steps. There are no wrong
   answers and it is not a medical test.

4. MINECRAFT
   Open "Gaming". The "Minecraft — Nether Visibility" profile is used
   automatically while Minecraft is in front. If the colors don't change in
   the game, play in windowed / maximized mode instead of F11 fullscreen.

5. WHAT IS THAT COLOR?
   Point the mouse at something and press  Ctrl + Alt + Shift + C
   The color name appears next to the mouse pointer.

IF THE SCREEN EVER LOOKS WRONG
   * Press  Ctrl + Alt + Shift + Backspace   (emergency reset), or
   * click "Reset colors" in the app, or
   * double-click "AkenoColorVision-EmergencyReset.exe" in this folder.
   The app then starts in safe mode next time (filter off until you turn it on).
   Hold Shift while starting the app to force safe mode.

PRIVACY
   Works completely offline. No account, no telemetry. Your screen is never
   recorded or uploaded. Profiles stay on this PC
   (%LOCALAPPDATA%\AkenoColorVision).
